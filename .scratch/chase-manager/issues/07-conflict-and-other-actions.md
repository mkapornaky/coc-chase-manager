# 07: Conflict and other actions

**What to build:** The remaining turn options. Attack spends 1 movement action and is logged. "Fighting maneuver succeeded against X" applies the hazard-failure outcome to the target (1D3 lost actions, optional damage). Other action (spell, lock, firing while stopped) spends 1 movement action with a note. Delay moves a participant later in the round. Manual overrides let the Keeper set any participant's location, movement actions, or debt directly. An End chase button ends the chase. A sudden-hazard indicator shows whose turn it is to call one (Keeper or players) and toggles when a call is made.

**Blocked by:** 04 (Hazards and movement-action debt)

**Status:** ready-for-agent
